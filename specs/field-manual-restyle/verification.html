10 Defects found and fixed

Three real defects surfaced during the browser pass. All three were presentation-only and were fixed before this report.

DefectCauseFix
Page body scrolled sideways at 360px — 686px wide The flow diagram’s 640px floor widened its ancestors: the wrapping grid column and the diagram’s outer div both defaulted to min-width: auto, so overflow-x: auto never engaged. grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)] on the chapter grid, and min-w-0 on the diagram wrapper.
Tab strip visible below 760px, where the bottom nav should replace it The mobile hide rule was never applied to the strip. max-[759px]:hidden on the strip.
The “n pasal” floating button was untappable on mobile The back-to-top button sat at the same bottom-right offset and swallowed its taps. Raised back-to-top below 760px; verified the two no longer overlap and the fab scrolls the sheet into view.

No defect required a change to lib/. Every one was a class or layout fix in components/.

11 Fallback report

Each of the ten known planning-time candidates, confirmed or corrected.

01
prefers-reduced-motion is not honoured

confirmedA deliberate deviation from the brief, matching the repo’s documented unconditional-motion policy and commit 722b380. No reduced-motion block and no motion-safe / motion-reduce variants exist.

02
Chapter = category, not section

confirmed7 chapters over 37 sections. The TOC lines up 1:1 and every count is computed from the data, never hard-coded.

03
Two new colour values plus --on-tx

confirmedOnly --tx, --outline and --on-tx were added. The brief’s full palette was not adopted.

04
Margin columns are per-section, not per-chapter

confirmedBecause a chapter is a category, “below 980px the margin flows below” is implemented below the section.

05
Tier labels come from the legend block’s desc

confirmed5 groups; all 13 ranks appear exactly once; no label is invented.

06
Penal rows split on “ — ”

confirmedVerified against all 35 penal bullets — every one splits cleanly. A bullet without the separator degrades to a bare term.

07
Flow entry/hot nodes are the first node of each track

confirmed5 tracks, 28 nodes, 24 connectors; every label traces to the data.

08
The callout’s “Peringatan” label is a UI label

confirmedCallout blocks carry no title of their own, so the label is the site’s own word for the concept rather than invented content.

09
The cover’s intro paragraph was moved from Hero.tsx

confirmedCarried over verbatim before the file was deleted.

10
Copy button renamed to “Salin daftar pasal”

confirmedA UI label, matching the brief. The copied string itself is unchanged and still pinned by the tests.
